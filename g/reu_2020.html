Multiple NSF REU Positions Open for Summer 2020

My research group has multiple NSF Research Experience for Undergraduate (REU) positions open for Summer 2020. Successful applicants will be working with me and my students on video understanding and/or audio-visual analysis. The task will be reading and studying related papers, implementing machine learning and deep learning models, evaluating them through analysis and testing, and writing a final report to summarize your findings.

The REU period is 3 months. The choice of the three-month work dates is flexible. The total pay for the complete period (including the possibility of a paid travel to attend an academic conference) is about $8,000. Note that the REU position does not include any arrangements for housing. You must be a US citizen or a permanent resident in order to apply. If you are interested, email your CV and transcript to me with your email starting with "[NSF REU]" in the title. Feel free to share this ad with your friends and encourage them to apply.
